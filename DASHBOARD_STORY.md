# Urban Company Service Operations Dashboard Story

## Purpose

This document provides two concise management narratives based on the Urban Company service-operations dashboard.

Each narrative follows the required structure:

**Headline → Evidence → Implication**

The narratives focus on one City Operations insight and one Category performance insight.

---

# 1. City Operations Lead

## Headline

**Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate and needs operational attention.**

## Evidence

Bengaluru recorded **17 SLA breaches across 107 bookings**, resulting in an SLA breach rate of approximately **15.9%**.

The overall operation recorded **79 SLA breaches across 600 bookings**, resulting in an overall SLA breach rate of **13.2%**.

Therefore, Bengaluru's SLA breach rate is approximately **2.7 percentage points higher** than the overall rate.

## Implication

The City Operations team should investigate the causes and patterns behind Bengaluru's SLA breaches and prioritize corrective actions to improve service reliability.

---

# 2. Category Lead

## Headline

**Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest revenue contributor but requires SLA monitoring.**

## Evidence

Deep Home Cleaning generated **₹508,964 in revenue from 176 bookings**, making it the highest-revenue category in the dataset.

The category also recorded **21 SLA breaches**, which means its strong revenue performance should be considered together with its service-level performance.

## Implication

The Category Lead should maintain the strong revenue performance of Deep Home Cleaning while monitoring its SLA performance and investigating opportunities to reduce service-level breaches.

# 6. Management Takeaways

### City Operations

Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate at approximately **15.9%**, compared with the overall rate of **13.2%**. This makes Bengaluru the primary city for operational investigation.

### Category Performance

Deep Home Cleaning is the largest revenue contributor with **₹508,964 revenue and 176 bookings**. Its **21 SLA breaches** should be monitored while maintaining its strong revenue performance.

### Overall Operational View

The dashboard should be used to monitor revenue, booking volume, and SLA performance together rather than relying on revenue alone. This provides a more balanced view of service-operations performance.
